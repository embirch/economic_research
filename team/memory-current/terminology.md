# Current measurement conventions, 30 September 2026

Use the actual source definitions. Generative AI is narrower than all AI. Participation is not intensity, skill or benefit. Survey male/female categories are not a full gender-identity measure. Name-associated message shares are not respondent gender or population adoption. Task-inferred occupations and occupational sex composition do not reveal an individual user's gender. Exposure is not job loss or realised returns.

For the first article preserve its established language: absolute gap is male minus female use in percentage points; relative gap is that difference divided by male use, expressed as a percentage. Retain both underlying rates and female advantages. Do not import a new definition or sampling model from the alternative Claude analysis.

Anthropic-specific terms apply only when analysing that source; consult its source profile and economic-index-data skill. There is no rule that all findings say Claude.
