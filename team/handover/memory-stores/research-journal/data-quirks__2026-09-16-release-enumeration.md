## Source-fetch and publication quirks found while building the wiki (keywords: web_fetch, cdn.sanity.io, url_not_allowed, curl, appendix copies, alt text, contradictions)
- `web_fetch` refuses `cdn.sanity.io` URLs (`url_not_allowed`); `curl` retrieves them (HTTP 200). Use curl + pypdf for Anthropic appendix PDFs hosted there.
- The March 2026 (Learning curves) appendix exists in two builds: `f065…` (2026-03-23) and `a3cd…` (2026-03-24, later, linked from "Appendix — Available here"). Four textual differences, incl. the scope of the privacy-threshold rule ("In the analysis described in Chapter 2" vs "When we analyze log-level data"). Treat `a3cd…` as governing.
- Numbers that exist only in image alt text (e.g. Feb 2025 launch post: 3.4%/9.1% workforce shares, $60,070 median wage, 57.4/42.6 subtype shares) are recorded marked "(alt text)", never quoted as prose (room/director-2026-09-16-alt-text-ruling.md).
- Published-text contradictions to avoid quoting: (a) March 2025 report says no occupational category is automation-dominated, but its own figure/notebook give Protective Service 55.4% and Production 54.9% automation; (b) April 2025 software-development report gives the enterprise share of Claude Code conversations as 13% in the summary and 23.8% in the body; (c) Jan 2026 blog says Jan-2025 augmentation was 55%, the report says 56%; (d) Sept 2025 blog attributes finance use to New York, the report to Florida; blog rounds 9.3→12.4% as "more than 40 percent".
- v1 and v2 automation/augmentation files are on different bases (v1 sums to 84.2, v2 to ~100); renormalise over the five classified patterns before comparing.
- Release_2025_02_10: the 5-accounts/15-conversations task threshold IS applied before release (3,513 of 18,428 tasks present); the skill's "thresholds are not applied" is wrong for that wave.
- India brief: primitives N (58,098 of 975,160 = 5.96%) is inconsistent with the published 5.8% share; the 84.6% human-only-ability figure is glossed two opposite ways on the same page.
- Mentor (Massenkoff) is an acknowledged contributor on the India and Australia spotlights (authors Ruth Appel; Peter McCrory).
- Environment: `web.archive.org` bodies are blocked by egress policy (the availability API answers; snapshots do not). `web_fetch` on redirected anthropic.com URLs can return a cached pre-redirect rendering that differs from the live target (AI Fluency Index: footnote year 2025 vs 2026) — treat curl's live target as authoritative. bls.gov/download.bls.gov return 403 to the sandbox.
- AI Fluency Index: all 11 prevalence rates exist only in alt text; Anthropic's own term is "the artifact effect" (not "artifact paradox"); there is no composite index value.
- Anthropic Interviewer release: transcripts carry only transcript_id and text; none of the post's percentages are reproducible from it.
- PDF vs web divergences (ruling room/director-2026-09-16-pdf-web-ruling.md: PDF is document of record; record both, treat neither as governing): June 2026 report says sleep-advice peak "3 a.m." (PDF p.4) vs "5 a.m." (PDF preview bullet and web); Chapter 1 of June 2026 states no sample window (only Ch. 2: 10 Apr–10 Jun 2026); footnotes numbered per chapter in PDFs but continuously on web (Mar 2026, Jun 2026); Mar 2026 web cross-references a non-existent Figure 1.6 (PDF 1.5) and its PDF p.15 carries a stray production note ("Needs to be 3.42 and 3.40"); Mar 2026 tenure effect quoted in four sizes (10% / 6.4 pp / ~5 pp / ~3–4 pp).
