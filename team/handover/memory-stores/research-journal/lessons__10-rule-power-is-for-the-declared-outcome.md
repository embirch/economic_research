## Rule power is for the declared outcome, not the clause (keywords: power, ordered decision rule, H4, equivalence, pre-registration, post1)
Added 2026-09-17 (session 2.1). In an ordered decision chain (post1 §9(1): H1 → H2 → O-A → H4 → O-B), the power of a later step is not the power of its own clause: an earlier step can claim the draw first. The referee's brief-stage figure for H4 (80% up to ≈0.49 pp) was step (4)'s clause alone; H4 as declared, after step (3) takes every three-interval same-sign result, reaches 80% only up to ≈0.32 pp. The analyst caught it while writing P2; the full referee conceded in its prereg verdict. Rule: compute per-rule power by simulating the whole chain and counting the declared owner; state the declared-outcome figure in the prereg and footnote any brief figure that quoted the clause. Second lesson from the same review: an "any-wave" rule for a composition leg declares composition with no real composition in up to 60% of small-gradient draws; a "persistent-leg" rule cuts that to ≤2% but has power only 0.3–0.7 against a single-leg loss in the sub-margin range — the post must state both numbers.
