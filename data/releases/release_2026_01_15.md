## Grains

- **One row per `(geo_id, geography, date_start, date_end, platform_and_product, facet, level, variable, cluster_name)`.** Zero duplicates on that key in either file (V10). No shorter key is unique: `facet × variable × cluster_name` repeats across geographies, and `level` is needed because the `request` taxonomy reuses cluster names across levels.
- **Analytical grain by facet**: geography-level totals (`facet` = `country` / `country-state`, `cluster_name` empty); geography × category (`collaboration`, `use_case`, …); geography × distribution statistic for the numeric primitives; and global-only category × category intersections.
- **Units of observation**: a Claude.ai *conversation*; an API *prompt–response record* (report, Chapter 1 footnote 1). Occupation/task is inferred from the conversation, not from the user.
- Claude.ai geography counts: 1 global + 174 `country` ids (173 ISO-2 + `not_classified`) + 1,091 `country-state` ids = 1,266 distinct `geo_id` (V11). Sub-national rows exist for 135 parent countries; 110 of the 1,091 are `<ISO2>-not_classified` (0.8 % of sub-national usage) (V11, V12).
- The API file is a single geography: `geo_id = GLOBAL` in all 187,772 rows (V5).

## Metrics

Variable names are `{facet}_{statistic}` and `{base}_{secondary}_{statistic}` (V6):

- **Counts and shares**: `usage_count`, `usage_pct`; `{facet}_count`, `{facet}_pct`; intersections `{base}_{secondary}_count`, `{base}_{secondary}_pct`.
- **Distribution statistics for the five numeric primitives**, at every geography: `_mean`, `_median`, `_stdev`, `_count`, `_mean_ci_lower/upper`, `_median_ci_lower/upper` (8 variables per primitive per geography). At global only, additionally `_histogram_count` and `_histogram_pct` (10 variables) (V16).
- **Intersection statistics**: `onet_task_{primitive}_{mean|median|stdev|count|*_ci_*|pct}` (9 variables); `request_{primitive}_…` (7 — **no `median_ci_lower/upper`** for request intersections) (V16).
- **API-only**: `cost_index`, `prompt_tokens_index`, `completion_tokens_index`.

Bases, all verified to sum to 100 within a geography (V17):
`usage_pct` at `country` is a share of the **global** sample (sums to 100.000 over the 174 country
ids, including `not_classified`); `usage_pct` at `country-state` is a share of the **parent
country** (sums to 100.000 for each of the 135 parents); every `{facet}_pct` is a share of the
geography's total **including `not_classified`**; every intersection `_pct` is a share of its base
cluster.

## Thresholds

- The documentation states the minimum-observation rule: **200 conversations per country, 100 per country-state region**, and says explicitly it is "applied in enrichment step, not raw preprocessing". These files are raw: **the thresholds are not applied**. 55 of the 174 country ids fall below 200 (minimum 15), and 540 of the 1,091 sub-national ids fall below 100 (minimum 1) (V19). All 51 US states clear 100.
- The privacy floor **is** applied: the smallest `onet_task_count` at global is exactly 15, and the smallest bottom-up `request_count` is 523 — consistent with the report's ≥15 conversations / ≥5 accounts for cells and ≥500 conversations / ≥250 accounts for request clusters (V20). The floor is applied to *base* cells only: intersection cells go down to a count of 1 (`onet_task::collaboration`), and country-level `collaboration_count` goes down to 2 (V20).
- Anomaly rules the report applies and the file does not: **Seychelles excluded from all geographic analyses** (abusive traffic; its 24,715 conversations are 2.5 % of the global sample and give an AUI of 1,054.6) and **Wyoming excluded from all US state analyses** (same reason; AUI 2.073, third highest state) (V21, V22).
- Suppression is by omission, not by a flag: absent cells simply have no row. There is no suppression indicator column.

## Cuts that do not exist

Plainly, in this release:

1. **No `soc_occupation` facet at any geography**, and no SOC column anywhere. Occupation-group shares (the "34 % computer & mathematical") must be rebuilt by joining `onet_task` cluster names to `onet_task_statements.csv` from `release_2025_09_15`. The join is clean (3,168/3,168) but it is an external join, and it exists only where `onet_task` exists.
2. **No intersection at any geography below global.** `onet_task::*` and `request::*` are global-only. So: no automation-by-task by country, no use-case-by-task by state, no primitive-by-task by country. The September 2025 release had the same restriction for `onet_task::collaboration` and `request::collaboration`; this release extends it to all twenty intersections.
3. **No primitive histograms below global.** Countries and sub-national units get mean, median, stdev, count and both CIs — and nothing else. Distributional claims by geography are impossible; only moments are.
4. **No AI Usage Index, no population, no GDP, no income, no task-mix adjustment, no automation/augmentation bucket variable.** Every one of these must be rebuilt (see above) or taken from `release_2025_09_15`.
5. **No `usage_count` for the API file** — the API has no denominator of its own and no geography; every API number is a global share.
6. **No Claude Code, no Max flag, no Artifacts, no survey, no model or product breakdown, no user-level or account-level anything, no time series**: one seven-day window, one row per cell.
7. **No US state × O\*NET task for most states** in practice: 47 of 51 states have any task cell and the median state has 22 of 3,170 tasks. State-level task mixes are not usable as mixes.
8. **No `request` cluster hierarchy file** (the 2025-09-15 release shipped `request_hierarchy_tree_claude_ai.json`; this one does not), so the parent of a level-0 cluster is not recoverable from this folder.
9. **No `not_classified` population**, therefore no AUI for the 15.7 % of conversations whose country is unknown; they can only enter a denominator.
10. **No enriched file**, hence no ISO-3 codes and no `onet_task_pct_index` specialisation index despite the documentation describing it.

## Traps

1. **`NA` is Namibia.** The country list contains `NA`, and `NAM` never appears. Read every file with `keep_default_na=False` (V11).
2. **Units differ inside the same facet family.** `human_only_time` is in **hours**, `human_with_ai_time` is in **minutes** — the appendix prompts make this explicit (V3). The global means (3.09 vs 15.35) look like a contradiction until you convert; any speedup must be `hours × 60 ÷ minutes`.
3. **The automation share in this report uses all conversations as the base**, not the five classified patterns: 45.36 % reproduces the published 45 %, while the classified base gives 46.74 % and reproduces nothing. The base changed between waves: the August 2025 enriched file carries a `collaboration_automation_augmentation` facet whose `automation_pct` is 51.07 — the classified base, which is what the September 2025 report quoted. Same construct, two bases, two reports; check the base every wave (V26).
4. **The AUI country denominator includes `not_classified` usage but not its population**; the released code says otherwise and is contradicted by Anthropic's own published index (V25). States exclude `not_classified` from both.
5. **Seychelles is 2.5 % of the whole global sample** and has an AUI of 1,054.6; Wyoming is the third-highest state. Both are excluded by the report itself (Chapter 3 footnotes 5 and 6). Any global mix, any share, any denominator computed without excluding Seychelles is contaminated — including `usage_pct`, which the file publishes with Seychelles in it.
6. **Median confidence intervals do not bracket the median.** Global `human_only_time` median is 1.5 with CI [1.5665, 1.5753]; `ai_autonomy` median 4.0 with CI [3.943, 3.944]; `human_education_years` median 12 with CI [12.206, 12.213]. The medians are discrete; the CIs come from a bootstrap of a smoothed statistic. Never plot them as error bars around the median (V16). Some `*_median_ci_lower` values are negative (V9).
7. **`cluster_name` is an empty string, not null**, on aggregate rows — 48,730 of them. A `dropna()` or a default `read_csv` will either drop them or turn them into `NaN` and break a join.
8. **`level` is a string** and `request` cluster names recur across levels; filter on both or you will double-count.
9. **The thresholds are not applied.** 55 countries below 200 and 540 sub-national units below 100 are in the file, some with 1 conversation. Everything the report shows is thresholded.
10. **`platform_and_product` says "Claude AI (Free and Pro)" but the sample is Free, Pro *and* Max** — every appendix figure caption and Chapter 1 footnote 1 say so. The column label is wrong.
11. **Two different bases are used for occupation shares within the same report** (Chapter 1 all-conversation, Chapter 2 classified-only). Both reproduce; quoting one against the other looks like a discrepancy and is not.
