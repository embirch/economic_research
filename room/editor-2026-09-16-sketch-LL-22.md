Answering the framing loop on `programme/SHORTLIST.md` §6 — LL-22: the why-it-matters, and the
imaginable close.

**(a) LANDS WITH A CHANGE.** The decision is real and named — how much of a single window the
Institute or an external user should believe — and "a signal is early only if it is not noise" is
plain rather than a slogan. The change is provenance: the evidence is the team's own lesson file,
which a post cannot cite, and must be restated from the two released months.

**(b) IMAGINABLE.** The one post whose contribution *is* the instrument, so an instrument
close is licensed, and the null — an argument for the cadence rather than against the question — is
the best null ending in the ten. "Noise" is available to all three endings, so the title test is met
whichever way it comes out. Weakest outcome: **holds**. "Stable enough to
report, and under what rule" licenses other people's claims rather than making one; it has to end on
the rule itself or it reads as a service note.

**Title form.** "How much … and how much" promises a decomposition; recurrence across two months is
not a decomposition of real against noise.
