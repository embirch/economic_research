Answering the framing loop on `programme/SHORTLIST.md` §6 — LL-02: the why-it-matters, and the
imaginable close.

**(a) LANDS.** It names the instrument, the decision — which scenario the Institute treats as
central — and the prior, the middle scenario read as neutral. "One of the five is measured monthly by
the same company, and the measurement has never been carried to the model" is the corpus's
admitted-blind-spot opening in one sentence.

**(b) IMAGINABLE WITH A CHANGE.** The *null* is the strongest of the three: a measured analogue and
an assumed parameter not yet being the same kind of thing is a finding, and here the instrument is
the contribution, so an instrument close is licensed. Weakest outcome: **holds**. "The
company's own data sits at the gentle end of its own range of futures" is a characterisation, and it
is the ending the post's own first risk forbids writing plainly: if a conversation share is not a
wage-bill share, the close cannot rank presets without carrying that fence. It also drops the title's
word.

**Title form.** The question presupposes that observed use can support or fail an assumed parameter —
risk (1); the title concedes less than the design does.
