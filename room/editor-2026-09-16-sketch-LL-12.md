Answering the framing loop on `programme/SHORTLIST.md` §6 — LL-12.

**(a) LANDS.** "An Institute economist choosing between the two futures is currently choosing on a
statistic that cannot express one of them" is the plainest stake in the nine: decision, prior and the
defect in the existing measure, in one sentence. "The figure the corpus offers" is house
vocabulary and becomes the March 2026 appendix by name at drafting.

**(b) IMAGINABLE WITH A CHANGE.** Fails and null both work, and the null names what a stable taxonomy
would cost, which is a commitment rather than a request. Weakest outcome: **holds**, twice over.
"Each time we look" is first person. And a rising within-wave series does not show the cumulative
curve "was hiding" the reaching: a curve that cannot fall is uninformative about growth, not
concealing it, so a holds result licenses only that the published curve was right for a reason it
could not demonstrate.

**Title form.** "Recurring" is a word the design does not test — the second leg measures
concentration of task shares inside one window, not work recurring over time — and both can rise, so
the disjunction is not one.
