Answering the framing loop on `programme/SHORTLIST.md` §6 — LL-07: the why-it-matters, and the
imaginable close.

**(a) LANDS.** The strongest of the ten. The mechanism — automating expensive tasks moves a large
wage bill, automating cheap ones a small one — arrives before any number; the decision is named, the
weighting of every automation-share input; and the prior is exact, a reader who takes "automation
share" as if all tasks were the same size. "The corpus prices the work" is house vocabulary and must
become the two publications by name.

**(b) IMAGINABLE.** Holds and fails both carry the title's own words, say something an economist
would repeat, and need no number. Weakest outcome: the **null**. The sketch states that power is not
the constraint at 2,600 tasks, so the honest null is not "pricing a task and classifying a
conversation are too far apart to join" but "delegation does not track the price of the work" — which
is a finding and should be the ending; the join-failure version reads as a methods note. Both are writable in the register.

**Title form.** None. "Delegated", "cheap" and "expensive" are the corpus's own terms and all three
survive into the endings.
