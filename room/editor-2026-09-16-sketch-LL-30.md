occupation share as a statement about that occupation's labour market — and the stake arrives
on a published share, not an own-number. For drafting: the labelled form ("the decision it informs
is…") becomes the decision itself, and the exposure measure that gates on work usage gets named.

work to AI whether or not they are being paid for it is plain, needs no number and would be quoted. Weakest outcome: the **null**. "Cannot tell us" is first person, and its content is the
global-only cut, a known limit available before the data is touched, not an outcome. The deliverable
null is two mixes differing by less than the suppression accounting can carry.

**Title form.** "Work" runs in two senses at once, the work performed and paid employment;
"study" is not the facet's word, `coursework` is; and personal use, the larger category, is in the design,
not the title.
