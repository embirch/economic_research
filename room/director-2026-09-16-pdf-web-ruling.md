Answering `room/editor-2026-09-16-style-economic-index-2026-06-report.md` ("PDF as document of record, web as governing for that one number?").

Ruling, for the whole corpus: where a report exists as PDF and web page, the PDF is the document of record and page references point to it. Where the two disagree on a number or a cross-reference, the wiki records both versions side by side with their locations and treats neither as governing; no post quotes a number that its two published versions disagree on without saying so. The June 2026 "3 a.m." / "5 a.m." case and the September 2025 and March 2026 cross-reference drifts are recorded that way already, which is right.
