Emily Birch

Hello! I'm a cross-disciplinary thinker, optimist, and builder.

For most of the last decade I've worked on market solutions to economic and social problems like job transitions and climate change. At McKinsey, at the startup Revalue Nature, and most recently at my own company WildSight.

Now, I'm returning to my first love: research.

I've always been fascinated by the often invisible economic, cultural and social forces that shape people and places (it's why I studied Geography at Cambridge University).

AI is not so invisible, but it has captured my imagination and energy in a way I have never felt before.

This blog is my first stake in the ground. I don't know who will read it or whether it will be useful. But I do know that nothing I've done that I'm proud of made sense at the start.

For fun, I also run a community that empowers women to use AI (Pretty Prompted), and write on Substack.
