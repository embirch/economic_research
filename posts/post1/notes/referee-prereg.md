### (c) Power and intervals (P1, P2)

**8. Interval formulas, independence, MDE against δ. — CORRECT. Not blocking.**
Var(D) = Σ_{Q4}(w_i/W_4)² p_i(1−p_i)/n_i + Σ_{Q1}(w_i/W_1)² p_i(1−p_i)/n_i is the conversation-level binomial with weights fixed; Q1 and Q4 are disjoint task sets and a conversation maps to one task node (`onet_task_pct` sums to 100), so the two sums are independent, as P1 asked. Δ_W = Σ w·wage·p/Σ w·wage − Σ w·p/Σ w = Σ(a_i − b_i)p_i with a_i = w_i wage_i/Σ w wage, b_i = w_i/Σ w, and the identity Δ_W = Cov_w(wage, p)/E_w[wage] holds (E_w[wage·p]/E_w[wage] − E_w[p]); Var(Δ_W) = Σ(a_i − b_i)² p_i(1−p_i)/n_i follows. The general Cov(L, M) = Σ c_i e_i p_i(1−p_i)/n_i for linear statistics is right and is what licenses the D_L − ½D intervals. Per-wave MDE 0.42 / 0.42 / 0.43 pp against δ = 1 pp is informative; the prereg states plainly that a true D of exactly δ is *not* detected by H1 at 80% (0.000 at 1.0 pp; 80% from ≈ 1.5), which the post must carry.
