# Foundational synthesis: Cranney, Delecourt and Koning

Emily highlighted this report on 1 October 2026 while the v1 audit was running. It was already in the literature register as `CRANNEY_SYNTHESIS` and in the programme lead's reading assignment; its foundational role is now explicit.

The linked [HBS Working Paper 25-023](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/25-023_be8fb517-3dd5-40aa-97f9-4e42e1c8e6ff.pdf) is *Global Evidence on Gender Gaps and Generative AI Over Time*, by Katelyn Cranney, Solène Delecourt and Rembrand Koning, **May 2026**. Following Emily's instruction, Codex read the complete 65-page PDF on 1 October, including references, figures, source and measurement tables, and Appendices A and B. The [full-reading record and research implications](landscape-v1/coordination/HBS-READING.md) supersede the earlier selected-passage check; they do not claim independent verification of every cited primary study. Older imported notes describing it as “adoption only” are too narrow; retain those historical files but use this correction and the current specialist review.

Use it to orient discovery, trace primary studies and test what our programme adds. Its pooled study estimates and traffic measures are not automatically representative world-population prevalence. Keep study/sample overlap, weighting, denominator and proxy-gender limitations visible; independently check the evidence behind any numerical claim we use. The pooled analysis has not been reproduced here.

Our contribution must be assessed against this foundation. Maintain inspectable, source-specific indicators; investigate heterogeneity through our own original analyses; and extend the interpretation where verified evidence warrants it. These are intended contributions, not an established novelty claim. The first landscape should explicitly acknowledge this prior work and explain how newer or different findings relate to it.

Coverage correction, 1 October 2026: acknowledging this review as foundational has not yet been followed by a complete source-by-source incorporation of its 76-source corpus. The current 33 explorer cards are a partial, differently counted catalogue. Section 3.1 and Appendix A distinguish 58 plotted adoption entries and 18 narrative-review sources. All 76 need a recorded disposition alongside our additional sources; see the [reconciliation requirements](landscape-v1/coordination/COVERAGE-RECONCILIATION.md). Access to respondent data is not a prerequisite for keeping relevant published evidence in the literature library.
