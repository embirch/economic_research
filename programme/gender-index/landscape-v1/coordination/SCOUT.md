## Coverage and interpretation

The [search log](search-log.csv) records targeted searches and direct provider checks. It is a bounded discovery record, not a systematic review. English-language searches on Singapore and Saudi provider sites and English/French searches aimed at Egypt/Morocco did not establish a usable gender-by-GenAI table in this pass. That is not a finding of no available evidence. Local-language follow-up and other providers remain open; the Caribbean, Pacific islands, Central Asia and most individual countries have not been individually audited.

The current evidence favours investigating national modules alongside the European core. Even the UK, with a three-month reference window, differs in age range and AI definition. Definitions, populations and modes must be checked before interpreting apparent international differences. Digital access and exposure evidence may explain context but cannot stand in for observed GenAI use or worker outcomes.
