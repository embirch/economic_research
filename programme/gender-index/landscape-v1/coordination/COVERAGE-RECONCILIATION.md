# Coverage correction: initial 33 cards versus 76 HBS entries

Status update: the [entry reconciliation](HBS-RECONCILIATION.md) now accounts for all 76 and expands the explorer to 111 cards. The note below preserves the original diagnosis and requirements. Primary-source and sample-overlap checks remain open.

1 October 2026. Emily asked why the explorer contains only 33 records when the foundational HBS paper includes 76 sources. The current catalogue does not yet incorporate that full corpus. Describing the initial bounded delivery as sufficient completion of the broader landscape was premature.

## What the counts mean

The May 2026 [Cranney, Delecourt and Koning paper](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/25-023_be8fb517-3dd5-40aa-97f9-4e42e1c8e6ff.pdf), section 3.1 and Appendix A, reports 76 sources identified by 28 May 2026. It distinguishes 58 plotted adoption entries from 18 sources retained in its narrative review. Those 76 sources are not necessarily 76 independent, downloadable datasets.

Our 33 records are a mixture of datasets, indicator cards, literature and discovery leads. Eurostat, Brazil and Korea each have three cards for different measures. The HBS synthesis itself has one card (`CRANNEY_SYNTHESIS`); its underlying sources have not been individually reconciled against the programme register. Some are already represented or discussed in specialist prose, but that is not a complete source-level inventory. No exact overlap or missing-source count has yet been established. The 25 rows in the specialist's `literature-updates.csv` are field-level update proposals, not 25 additional studies.

The initial assignment started with 14 indicator candidates and 11 literature records, four named additions and eight further discovery slots. That stopping rule explains the partial catalogue. It did not implement a full expansion of the foundational review. The current cards are not the result of screening all 76 sources and retaining only the eligible ones.

## Required next work before selecting new deep dives

1. Account for every source in the HBS corpus using stable source IDs and the appendix location. Retain a link to its HBS entry; record any uncertainty about citation identity. Initially label information extracted from HBS as review-derived, not independently checked against the primary study.
2. Reconcile each entry with existing programme records and the specialist literature leads. Separate publications, underlying datasets/sample families, waves and outcomes. Do not double-count the synthesis and its constituent studies as independent evidence or add 76 and 33 mechanically.
3. Add verified distinct sources outside that corpus, including newer research and sources about experience, mechanisms, workplace conditions, exposure and realised benefits or harms. Record publication and fieldwork dates separately and document search scope.
4. Maintain separate counts for literature sources, underlying datasets/sample families, checked gender-specific measures and datasets feasible for our own analysis. Retain useful published findings and mechanism evidence even when respondent data are unavailable; catalogue inclusion is different from eligibility for numerical comparison or reanalysis.
5. Show an explicit disposition for each HBS entry: already matched, added, overlapping, unresolved or excluded with a reason. Report reconciliation progress against all 76 entries and explain any exclusions before claiming the broader landscape is sufficiently mapped for paper selection.

The expected evidence library should extend beyond the HBS corpus where distinct relevant additions exist. Its exact size follows reconciliation, not a target number. The comparable indicator and reanalysis-ready subsets may be much smaller. The existing checked panels remain useful, but their validation does not establish completeness of the literature.

This note corrects programme scope and display wording. It does not claim that the 76-source reconciliation has been performed or authorise another paid session. The live author manuscript remains outside this correction's edit scope.
