# Proposed method for the three research posts

9 October 2026. Proposal for Emily's review, not an approved paid assignment or a readiness verdict. Prepared from the current local manuscripts, project instructions, previous editorial records, selected Anthropic primary articles and a separate native Codex critique of this method. No manuscript or empirical output changed; no paid session launched.

## Objective and diagnosis

Produce three standalone articles that an exacting economics reader can follow without supplying missing definitions, mechanisms or connections. The reference audience includes Maxim Massenkoff. This means learning from published explanatory choices, not predicting his judgement or implying endorsement.

The completed calculations are the foundation. They do not establish that the articles communicate useful research. Earlier review plans already requested stronger motivation, early answers, synthesis and cold reading. Repeating those instructions with another model would leave the same failure possible. The proposed change is to require evidence of what the article actually communicates, independently of its author's explanation of what it was meant to communicate.

One accountable editor should own each complete article. Begin with Canada and establish the standard on a full proposed redraft, including its results sections and figures. Rewriting only the opening or Introduction–Discussion pair risks leaving the central argument disconnected.

The articles may warrant different lengths and forms: an empirical article for Canada, a focused empirical note for Brazil, and an applied methods note for the US. Stronger writing must not imply equal novelty, causal identification or breadth of contribution.

## 1. Establish the argument and its evidence before polishing

The managed Claude Editor receives a developmental assignment, not a request for cosmetic copyediting. It first prepares a short internal argument brief, then writes the entire proposed article from that brief. Codex checks the brief against existing evidence and adjudicates the final proposal.

The brief must answer, in ordinary language:

- What economic question makes this worth reading, and why does gender matter specifically?
- What does this dataset actually observe, for whom, when, and under which definition?
- What exact comparison can answer the narrower empirical question?
- What is the principal answer, and what do the findings together add beyond the separate statistics?
- Which established finding or interpretation does this extend, qualify or challenge?
- What plausible alternative interpretation remains, and how does it change the conclusion?

Separate an economic motivation from a measured mechanism. A statement about what AI could change cannot silently become a claim about what these data show it changed. If an essential bridge needs evidence, identify the missing support before writing around it.

The result is not a rigid prose template. Paragraphs should follow the needs of the argument. Preserve Introduction, the exact research questions once, and Discussion before Methodology, as currently requested. Definitions essential to understanding a finding belong where it is introduced; technical implementation detail can remain in Methodology.

## 3. Build synthesis with sources that perform a clear job

For every substantive interpretive step, connect the proposed sentence to the relevant existing result or primary-source passage. Record what the evidence supports and what it cannot support. Reuse the current literature notes and completed verification; do not rebuild the corpus.

Supporting literature should establish the stakes, clarify a mechanism, provide a fair comparison or challenge an interpretation. A citation at the end of a paragraph does not repair an unexplained inference. Keep differences in populations, outcomes and designs visible when comparing studies. Treat relevant Cranney, Delecourt and Koning claims explicitly in the internal evidence brief, preserving the existing reading-depth and source-lineage distinctions.

Targeted primary-source checking is appropriate where a proposed sentence relies on an unread passage or a different version. Do not upgrade an abstract reading to a full-paper reading. Where support is unavailable, narrow or remove the claim. New data acquisition, new models and a reopened novelty search are outside this editorial assignment.

| Article | Explanatory burden | Unacceptable inference |
|---|---|---|
| Canada, E08 | Explain unpaid job work as an aspect of employees' experience; distinguish a user–nonuser association from changes after adoption; explain why observed job distributions and a common mix answer different descriptive questions. Connect the gender motivation to those questions. | AI raises working hours, women receive fewer causal benefits, jobs explain away the difference, or the common-mix ordering is a stable reversal. |
| Brazil, E03 | Explain what same-person combinations add beyond separate, already high reason frequencies. Put conditional shares beside the existing same-sample baselines. Explain the uncertainty in the sex comparison. | Co-reported reasons identify causes, complementary interventions, demand for training, equal needs, or an explanation of the overall adoption gap. |
| US, E10 | Explain the two roles of the occupation rule, the paired numerators and different denominators, and fixed-worker versus expanded-worker comparisons before interpreting the sign change. Show how another researcher could use the audit. | A new estimator, a uniquely correct classification, a unique causal decomposition, or identified household financial vulnerability. |

Each figure needs a stated argumentative purpose. The adjacent prose must tell readers what is being compared, what to notice and how it changes the interpretation. Essential labels and uncertainty cannot depend on finding another tab or appendix. Existing numerical outputs remain fixed unless a specific error is established.

## 4. Apply the sentence test throughout the complete draft

The Canadian opening is a calibration example, not the only paragraph to repair. The editor must identify the exact ambiguity and replace it with an explicit, defensible connection.

| Observed defect | Required repair test |
|---|---|
| AI is absent from the opening. | Does the opening identify the subject and the specific economic question? |
| “Working less” can mean hours, effort or output. | Is the outcome named precisely, and does it stay consistent through the paragraph? |
| A shorter day, more output and different responsibilities are presented as comparable alternatives. | Are distinct dimensions separated? Can outcomes occur together? Are the conditions for the example stated? |
| “Depends on demand” stands in for a mechanism. | Who does what differently, under what condition, and how does that affect the worker? |
| Motivation concerns hours while the survey measures frequency of unpaid job work. | Does the article explain the narrower measurement and its value, without treating it as hours or causal time savings? |

Apply equivalent scrutiny to every central paragraph and transition. Words such as “therefore”, “suggests” and “matters” must have an explicit logical basis. Concrete hypothetical examples may explain a mechanism if clearly labelled; they must not be presented as observed respondents or established behaviour.

The Discussion must integrate the findings into a substantive answer before discussing further research. A conclusion consisting only of “adoption is not enough” or “we need better data” is insufficient. Qualifications should sharpen the answer rather than replace it. Headings, chart titles and conclusions must make the same claim at appropriate levels of detail.

## 5. Test what a fresh reader actually understood

After Emily has seen the proposed Canadian draft, use a genuinely fresh native Codex reader with no inherited conversation history. Supply only the proposed standalone article package, including its figures and reader-facing methods, plus the reading task. Do not supply previous reviews, the editor's argument brief, a desired answer, or the internal claims dossier. Record the exact input files and hashes. This limits supplied context; it is not a claim of a blinded human experiment or a technical access-control boundary.

Before seeing the supporting dossier, the reader must write:

1. The question, stakes, measured population and comparison in its own words.
2. The answer and the contribution it understood.
3. Why each central figure matters and how the result leads to the conclusion.
4. The main alternative explanation and the inference the design cannot support.
5. Every passage it had to reread, ambiguous term and missing connection.

Every answer must point to an actual passage. The reader must distinguish what the article states from what it supplied using background knowledge. A correct explanation produced by filling in a central missing bridge is an editorial failure. Freeze this first-read record before the evidence review. Do not count repeated tests on the same reader as independent fresh readings.

This native stage is explicitly separate from the paid Claude specialists. It is useful because the existing paid runner submits one prompt to a repository-backed session; supplying the full evidence packet immediately and merely asking that agent to ignore it would weaken the first-reader test.

## 6. Managed Claude referee, correction and final checks

Commission the configured Claude Referee in a new session for each article. Give it the exact draft, figures, first-read record and complete relevant evidence packet. Do not provide an instruction to obtain a passing verdict. It checks title, contribution, reasoning, definitions, primary-source interpretation, uncertainty, denominators, captions and conclusions against the saved results and methods.

This is a draft-and-claims review. Completed numerical reconstructions, including Canada's all-16-stratum influence check, are reused. Recompute only to resolve a concrete discrepancy; a proposed substantive analytical extension must be reported separately. The referee may conclude that the defensible contribution is narrower than the requested framing.

Each objection needs a passage, an explanation of the defect, its consequence and a required resolution. Codex remains integration owner and performs one bounded correction cycle, preserving voice and checking the consequences across the whole article. Referee wording is not automatically copied into prose.

Use the configured second-read referee only when material corrections require a further evidence/logic verdict. It checks the resulting version and potential regressions, not merely whether requested phrases were inserted. If a central defect survives, retain a specific unresolved status rather than launch another cycle automatically. A budget or iteration limit never converts failure into readiness.

## 7. Acceptance and delivery

A paper fails the proposed standard if its motivating promise exceeds its design; if a central explanation depends on an unstated connection; if ordinary reading changes the outcome, population or causal meaning; if a figure and narrative imply different claims; or if its conclusion adds no supported insight beyond a list of results and generic calls for research.

Keep three separate version-specific judgements: explanatory coherence, evidential support, and faithful delivery. Passing one does not compensate for failing another. Do not average scores, count agent votes or treat a checklist as proof of quality. Codex is accountable for the integrated judgement; Emily retains editorial and publication decisions.

Only after the content is settled, rebuild HTML, PDFs and reproduction packages using the existing builders. Check changed prose/captions against unchanged numerical outputs, inspect the actual rendered reading sequence and figures, verify links, and ensure the reviewed version matches every delivered format. Preserve previous editions and all author edits. The deliverable includes the clean proposed articles, concise substantive-change notes, first-read records, resolved/unresolved referee items and final version hashes.
