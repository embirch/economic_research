## What each entry is (one line each)

- `economic-index-2025-02-report` — The launch post for the Index and its first report: O*NET task shares, the augmentation/automation taxonomy and the wage gradient, on Claude.ai Free and Pro.
- `economic-index-2025-02-paper` — The academic paper behind the first report; carries the sample windows, the privacy thresholds, the Job Zone table and the Appendix B classifier validation.
- `economic-index-2025-03-report` — Second report: re-measurement 11 days after the Claude 3.7 Sonnet launch, plus the release of the 630-cluster bottom-up usage taxonomy.
- `economic-index-2025-04-software-development` — Special report comparing Claude Code with Claude.ai coding conversations; the first Anthropic measurement of an agentic surface.
- `economic-index-2025-09-report` — Third report: introduces the AI Usage Index, US states and 150+ countries, and the first-party API sample.
- `economic-index-2025-09-blog` — The short companion post to the third report, written for a general reader; the source of the "overrepresented local uses" framing.
- `economic-index-2026-01-report` — Fourth report: introduces the five economic primitives, task success, task horizons, effective coverage and the state convergence model.
- `economic-index-2026-01-blog` — The short companion post to the fourth report, introducing the primitives to a general reader.
- `economic-index-2026-03-report` — Fifth report: tenure and learning-by-doing, model selection by task wage, and the revised convergence horizon.
- `economic-index-2026-03-appendix` — Separate appendix to the fifth report: primitive prompts, occupational figures and the cumulative-coverage figure. Exists in two builds that differ in wording; the later one is now the primary URL.
- `economic-index-2026-06-report` — Sixth report: hourly sampling and time-of-day cadences, the artifacts classifier, tokens against wages, and the first Economic Index Survey findings.
- `economic-index-2026-06-appendix` — Separate appendix to the sixth report: survey instrument, artifact taxonomy and robustness tables.
- `country-brief-india-2026-02` — Country spotlight on India built from the fourth report's November 2025 sample; India is the second-largest source of Claude.ai use.
- `country-report-australia-2026-03` — Country spotlight on Australia: state-level AUI, task diversity against Anglosphere peers, and the income-versus-composition question.
- `country-report-canada-2026-07` — Country spotlight on Canada: provincial AUI, the translation/bilingualism finding, and the coursework tilt.
- `labor-market-impacts-2026-03` — Introduces observed exposure (capability × usage × automation weighting) and tests it against BLS projections, CPS unemployment and youth hiring.
- `labor-market-impacts-2026-03-appendix` — The formal definition of the exposure measure, the usage gate, the α automation factor, and the measure-variant robustness.
- `productivity-gains-2025-11` — Claude-as-estimator time savings from 100k conversations aggregated by Hulten's theorem into an economy-wide productivity number.
- `claude-code-expertise-2026-06` — ~400k Claude Code sessions: work modes, decision attribution, the expertise gradient in autonomy and success, and rising task value.
- `claude-code-expertise-2026-06-appendix` — Classifier prompts, sample construction, the task-value estimator calibration and the internal-commit validation.
- `coding-agents-social-sciences-2026-05` — Baseline survey of 1,260 quantitative social scientists on coding-agent adoption and early research-output differences.
- `coding-agents-social-sciences-2026-05-appendix` — Recruitment, the regression specification and the pilot-versus-main selection check.
- `worker-retraining-2026-08` — Meta-analysis of 146 impact estimates from 56 US randomised trials of job training, with benefit-cost analysis and the sector-programme result.
- `skill-formation-rct-2026-01` — Randomised controlled trial with software developers on whether AI assistance changes skill formation and code understanding.
